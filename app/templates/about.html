Our Mission

Bridging the Gap to
Engineering Excellence

Preper was founded on a simple realization: the path to elite engineering institutions is often clouded by uncertainty and inconsistent resources. We exist to simplify that journey, providing every student with the tools, mentorship, and community needed to excel.

Why Preper Exists?

The NSAT is more than just a test; it's a gateway to a world of opportunity. Yet, many talented students miss out simply because they don't have access to real insights from those who have successfully crossed the bridge.

We've curated a platform that combines structured learning, high-stakes mock environments, and direct mentorship from NST students themselves. Our focus isn't just on solving problems, but on building the engineering mindset.
