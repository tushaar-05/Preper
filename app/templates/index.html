Frequently Asked Questions

What is the refund policy?

We have a strict no-refund and no-cancellation policy. Please review the course details carefully before making a purchase.

Once enrolled, you can access all study materials, mock tests, and lectures directly from your student dashboard.

Yes! Once you enroll in a batch, you get **lifetime access** to all the course materials, recordings, and resources.
